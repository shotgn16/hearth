# Hearth

Household money manager for Home Assistant, with local AI.

- Connect your bank (any SimpleFIN feed — Synci for UK banks, superFin, SimpleFIN Bridge — or any JSON feed) or import years of CSV/OFX/QIF statements
- Automatic categories that learn from every correction; subscriptions and bills spotted for you
- Regular payments tracker: payment dates, this month's checklist ticked off as payments come out, late-payment alerts, a 12-month history grid
- Email receipts: reads receipts and PDF bills from your mailbox (IMAP, Outlook.com / Microsoft 365, Exchange) and matches them to bills and payments for you to approve
- Budgets and savings targets — shared, per person, or for the kids; unexpected costs and work expense claims
- Insights, year-on-year trends and forecasts for weeks, months and years ahead
- Year planner and pivot reports that replace your spreadsheet
- Budgets and real costs on your Home Assistant, Outlook or iCal calendars
- Workflows that notify your phones through HA, fire events or call any HA service
- Ask your own Ollama model about your money: it looks up real figures and suggests budgets, goals and price watches you confirm with a tap
- Receipt scanning with a local vision model, monthly AI reviews
- Sensors for dashboards: free to spend, spent this month, balance, things to check, next payment

All data stays on your Home Assistant machine. Bank, mailbox and calendar logins and receipt files are encrypted at rest.
