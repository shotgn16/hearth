# Hearth

Household money manager for Home Assistant, with local AI.

- Connect your bank (superFin/SimpleFIN or any JSON feed) or import years of CSV/OFX/QIF statements
- Automatic categories that learn from every correction; subscriptions and bills spotted for you
- Budgets and savings targets — shared, per person, or for the kids
- Insights, year-on-year trends and forecasts for weeks, months and years ahead
- Year planner and pivot reports that replace your spreadsheet
- Budgets and real costs on your Home Assistant calendars
- Workflows that notify your phones through HA, fire events or call any HA service
- Ask your own Ollama model about your money: it looks up real figures and suggests budgets, goals and price watches you confirm with a tap
- Receipt scanning with a local vision model, monthly AI reviews
- Sensors for dashboards: free to spend, spent this month, next payment

All data stays on your Home Assistant machine.
